MARKETING RESEARCH · IN PROGRESS

Tampa Bay Times

An exploratory marketing research project focused on the St. Pete Beacon and readers' perceptions, experiences, and intention to read the publication.

01 — PROJECT

Research Context

This project explores how residents experience and perceive the St. Pete Beacon newspaper. The research is based on an exploratory survey that collected 71 responses.

The analysis looks at readership frequency, open-ended responses, recurring themes, and the factors that may influence readers' intention to read the newspaper.

02 — MY CONTRIBUTION

Research Process

Frequency Analysis

Analyzed readership frequency to understand how often respondents read the St. Pete Beacon.

Thematic Analysis

Reviewed open-ended responses and identified recurring ideas, opinions, and experiences.

Theme Classification

Classified the identified themes according to whether they could influence customer behavior or require operational action.

Hypothesis Development

Translated customer value drivers into measurable variables and developed hypotheses for Phase 2.

04 — THEMATIC ANALYSIS

What Are Readers Saying?

The open-ended survey responses were reviewed to identify recurring themes in respondents' experiences with the St. Pete Beacon.

Local Value & Community Engagement

Responses highlighted local news, local events, relevant information, positive stories, appealing layout, photography, and advertising.

Repetitive News & Different Column Events

Some responses discussed repetitive news while also identifying interest in different columns and local event coverage.

Distribution, Inaccessibility & Unawareness

Some responses reflected uncertainty about where the publication is available, delivery limitations, and accessibility issues.

07 — HYPOTHESES

From Research Themes to Hypotheses

The exploratory analysis was used to develop two hypotheses connecting perceived value drivers with residents' intention to read the St. Pete Beacon.

HYPOTHESIS 01

Local Value & Community Connection

Residents who perceive the St. Pete Beacon as providing greater local value and community connection will report a higher intention to read the newspaper.

Independent Variable

Perceived local value and community connection

Dependent Variable

Intention to read the St. Pete Beacon

Expected Relationship

Positive association

HYPOTHESIS 02

Content Variety & Differentiation

Residents who perceive the St. Pete Beacon as offering greater content variety and differentiation will report a higher intention to read the newspaper.

Independent Variable

Perceived content variety and differentiation

Dependent Variable

Intention to read the St. Pete Beacon

Expected Relationship

Positive association
